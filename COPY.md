## ICP blurb

You’re GST-registered. You file GSTR-1 / 3B (monthly or QRMP). Bills live in WhatsApp, email, and Drive. Your CA’s fee is mostly collation. You fear 2B mismatches and notices — and you don’t want to rebuild books in Zoho.

---

## Three pains

1. **Receipt chaos** — Screenshots and PDFs everywhere; CA bills ₹750–5k/mo largely to organise them.
2. **GSTR-2B recon hell** — Invoice-level Excel matching, late vendors, ITC timing stress.
3. **Notice fear** — 1↔3B mismatches, short reply windows, expensive emergency CA/advocate time.

---

## How it works (3 steps)

1. **Send** — Forward invoices from WhatsApp / email (or drop a ZIP / CSV).
2. **Match** — AI drafts books fields + flags GSTR-1 / 3B / 2B exceptions for you to confirm.
3. **Share** — One link packet for your CA (they keep the filing fee; you cut collation time).
