# ICP — GST Exception Pack

**Updated:** 2026-10-02 (Asia/Calcutta)  
**Source:** `research/2026-10-02-gst-rental/` (social + official desk research only; no customer interviews yet)

## Primary ICP

| Attribute | Target |
|-----------|--------|
| Who | Freelancer, small agency, or trader |
| GST | Registered (files GSTR-1 / GSTR-3B) |
| Turnover | Typically under **₹5 Cr** AATO — below e-invoice mandate pressure for many, still monthly/QRMP filing pain |
| Geography | India national; founder in Bengaluru |
| Tools today | WhatsApp/email bill chaos + CA; maybe Zoho Free / Tally / Excel — **not** looking to rip-and-replace books |
| Spend signal | Already paying CA **₹750–₹5,000/mo** largely for collation, or shocked by ₹60k annual CA bundles |

## Jobs to be done

1. Turn scattered invoices (WhatsApp / email / Drive) into a **GSTR-ready exception pack**.
2. See **purchase vs GSTR-2B** mismatches without Excel hell.
3. Hand a **shareable packet** to their CA (CA keeps filing fee; we cut collation time).
4. Optional later: notice reply **scaffold** (not legal advice).

## Pains (top 3 from research)

1. **Receipt / data chaos** — CA bill is often organising screenshots & bills, not the 20-min upload.
2. **GSTR-1 ↔ 3B ↔ 2A/2B recon** — invoice-level matching, vendor delays, ITC timing.
3. **Notice fear** — 1↔3B mismatch notices, 30-day windows, Section 73/74 panic.

## Anti-ICP (do not chase first)

- Full accounting suite buyers who want Tally/Zoho/ClearTax clone
- Enterprise multi-GSTIN / e-way IRP / auto-submit to GSTN
- Anyone needing legal advice or CA sign-off from us
- Rental-trust / PropTech (separate parked theme)
